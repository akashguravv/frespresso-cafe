Our story

Brewing on Station Road since 2017

We opened our doors in 2017 on the ground floor of Orchid Complex, opposite the Mayor’s Bungalow, with a simple idea: a place where Solapur could slow down over a good cup.

Today the menu runs from Italian coffees to pizzas, burgers, waffles and sundaes, an Indo-Western mix at around ₹200 to ₹400 per person. The vibe is still the same: easy, friendly and made for hanging out.

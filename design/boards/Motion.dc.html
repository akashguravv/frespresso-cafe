Only transform & opacity

Animate GPU-friendly properties only. Header height change can use a scaleY-free approach: swap padding with a 300 ms transition.

Reduced motion

Under prefers-reduced-motion: reveals become 200 ms fades, no Ken Burns, no steam loop, counters show final values.
